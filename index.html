Diamond & Jewellery Guide

Buying fine jewellery is emotional — and it should also be informed. Start by deciding the occasion (daily, festive, bridal), then the metal (our pieces are 925 sterling silver with rhodium finish), and only then the stone. Check hallmarks before price, ask for the stone's origin, and always buy from a maker who offers lifetime service.

  • Decide occasion → metal → stone, in that order
  • Verify hallmark & maker's code first
  • Ask for stone certificate on pieces above ₹2,000
  • Prefer makers with lifetime re-polish/service

Jewellery Care Guide

Silver loves to be worn — natural oils keep it bright. But it dislikes chemistry. Follow these rituals and your Parshiv piece will outlive trends.

Do

  • ✓ Wear often; wipe with the provided cloth after use
  • ✓ Store in the anti-tarnish pouch, sealed
  • ✓ Put jewellery on last, after perfume & makeup
  • ✓ Clean gently with lukewarm water + soft brush

Don't

  • ✗ Hot tubs, pools & sea water
  • ✗ Perfume, hairspray, household cleaners
  • ✗ Leave in humid bathrooms
  • ✗ Wear during gym / heavy lifting

Diamond Guide — the 4 Cs

Cut

The most important C — a well-cut stone sparkles even at lower colour/clarity. Prefer Excellent/Very Good cut.

Colour

Graded D (colourless) to Z. For silver settings, F–H near-colourless stones look identical face-up.

Clarity

VS–SI stones are "eye-clean" — inclusions invisible without 10× magnification. Smart value zone.

Carat

Weight, not size. A 0.90ct stone looks like 1.00ct but costs far less — ask us for such smart picks.

Certification Guide

Every Parshiv jewel carries the BIS hallmark — four marks: the BIS logo, purity 925, our maker's code, and the assay centre mark. Gem-set pieces above ₹2,000 include a stone certificate. Verify any hallmark via the BIS CARE app using the HUID number on your tag.

  • BIS logo + 925 purity stamp on every piece
  • Maker's code & assay centre mark
  • HUID number verifiable on BIS CARE app
  • Stone certificates (IGI/GIL) on gem pieces
